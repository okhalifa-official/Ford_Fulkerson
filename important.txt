Strategy: Greedy (augmenting path)
Algorithm: Ford-Fulkerson
Problem: Maximum Flow in Network

Real-life applications:
    - Bipartite Graph Matching
    - Data flow in network
    - Flow distribution
    - Image segmentation (separate background from foreground)
    - Efficient transportation flow
    - Chemical reaction pathways

Complexity:
    Space: O(V+E)
    Time: O(VE²)
